Remain inside the baggage reclaim hall and locate the airline's Baggage Service Office (or handling agent counter). File a Property Irregularity Report (PIR) immediately before passing customs. When you check in your luggage at the airport counter or self-service drop, an agent hands you a small barcode sticker stuck either to the back of your physical boarding pass or your passport cover. In this simulator, tracking tag numbers follow the standard format: a 3-letter IATA airport code followed by a hyphen and 6 digits (e.g. LHR-123456, DXB-309481). Digital boarding passes in airline apps also reflect this under "Checked Bags".

Standard international economy allowances generally permit one or two bags up to 23 kg (50 lbs) each, with total linear dimensions (Length + Width + Height) not exceeding 158 cm (62 inches). Business and First class allowances frequently permit bags up to 32 kg (70 lbs). Any individual bag exceeding 32 kg must be dispatched via specialized air freight due to occupational safety regulations.

"Flight in Transit" (Stage 4) confirms your bag was positively matched with the passenger manifest, loaded into the aircraft's belly hold or ULD container, and the aircraft has departed. During cruising flight, no ground barcode scanners interact with the bag until the aircraft touches down and apron unloading begins.

If your journey was booked as a single ticket through a hub airport (e.g., LHR to FRA, then connecting onward to SIN), your bag is tagged all the way to your final destination. At the intermediate transfer hub, ground teams offload "transfer" containers and run them through high-speed automated transfer sorters without requiring you to collect and re-check your bags.

Yes. Fragile and out-of-gauge items (bicycles, surfboards, cellos, golf clubs) must be taken to the dedicated "Oversize / Out-of-Gauge Baggage" drop counter. For domestic and narrow-body flights, first bags typically emerge within 15–25 minutes of wheel touchdown. For wide-body international flights (such as A380 or B777 with hundreds of checked bags), carousel delivery may take between 25 to 45 minutes. Priority-tagged bags (Business/First/Star Alliance Gold/SkyTeam Elite) are unloaded first.

A PIR is an official document generated across international WorldTracer systems documenting a baggage mishap (e.g. AS-2026-881923). It contains your delivery address, phone number, and physical suitcase attributes (brand, color, wheels, hard/soft shell). 98.5% of delayed bags are located and delivered to passengers' residences within 24 to 48 hours.

If automated computed tomography (CT) scanners detect an unresolved organic density or potential prohibited item (such as power banks, loose lithium batteries, or uncertified electronics), security personnel are authorized to conduct a physical inspection. Officers place an official TSA or national security notice inside your suitcase detailing the inspection.

Under the Montreal Convention (1999), airlines are legally liable for proven losses up to approximately 1,288 Special Drawing Rights (SDR) — roughly $1,700 USD — per passenger for lost or damaged luggage. For delayed baggage away from home, airlines routinely reimburse necessary emergency toiletries and clothing purchases upon submission of receipts.
