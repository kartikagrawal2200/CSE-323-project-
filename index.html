Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about baggage tags and arrival delivery.

  • Where can I find my baggage tag number?

    When you check in at the counter or bag drop, a sticker receipt with a barcode (tag format: 3 airport letters and 6 digits, e.g. LHR-123456) is handed to you or attached to your boarding pass.

    Read full answer →

  • What does "Flight in Transit" status indicate?

    Stage 4 confirms your suitcase has been positively matched with the passenger manifest, loaded into the aircraft hold, and the flight has departed.

    Read full answer →

  • What if my bag does not appear on the carousel?

    Immediately visit the Baggage Service Office inside the reclaim hall to file a Property Irregularity Report (PIR), or file an instant claim on AeroSim.

    Read full answer →
