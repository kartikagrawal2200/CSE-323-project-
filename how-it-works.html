IATA Standard Baggage Lifecycle

How Airport Baggage Handling Works

Follow your luggage through all six stages of the modern automated baggage journey.

Stage 1
Avg. Duration: 15–30 mins

1. Check-In Complete

When you arrive at the airport terminal or self-service drop, your bag is weighed, measured, and issued an official barcode tracking tag with a 3-letter airport prefix and 6 digits (such as LHR-123456). The tag encodes your flight number, routing, and passenger name record (PNR).

  • Automated scale verification and bag weight logging
  • High-adhesion barcode and RFID strap application
  • Transfer to behind-the-wall takeaway belt

Stage 2
Avg. Duration: 20–45 mins

2. Baggage Handling

Behind the secure terminal wall, your bag travels on miles of automated high-speed conveyor belts. Laser scanner arrays read the tag from 360 degrees, while multi-level computed tomography (CT) X-ray scanners inspect contents without opening the luggage.

  • Level 1 to Level 5 automated explosives detection screening
  • Optical tag recognition (OCR) and tilt-tray sorters
  • Routing to the designated flight makeup carousel

Stage 3
Avg. Duration: 30–50 mins

3. Aircraft Loading

Baggage handlers load your luggage into standardized aluminum Unit Load Devices (ULD containers) or onto baggage tugs for narrow-body aircraft. Handheld scanners confirm every bag matches a checked-in, boarded passenger (Positive Passenger Bag Match - PPBM).

  • Strict ICAO Positive Passenger Baggage Matching reconciliation
  • Weight and balance load sheet computation for flight stability
  • Hydraulic belt-loader hoisting into the aircraft lower deck hold

Stage 4
Duration: Scheduled flight time

4. Flight In Transit

While in the air, your baggage rests safely in pressurized, temperature-regulated cargo compartments. The airline's operations database tracks transit status in real time and sends preliminary manifest data to the destination airport's ground teams.

  • Fire-suppression and climate-controlled cargo compartments
  • Real-time ACARS manifest transmissions to arrival dispatch
  • Automated connection management for transferring bags

Stage 5
Avg. Duration: 15–25 mins

5. Aircraft Unloading

Immediately following touchdown and aircraft gate docking, the ground apron crew marshals container dollies to the aircraft hold. Bags are unloaded in priority sequence (First Class, Priority, Standard, followed by Cargo).

  • Immediate ramp offloading with barcode scan acknowledgment
  • Tug transfer from aircraft stand to terminal arrival induction
  • Customs screening channel routing for international arrivals

Stage 6
Avg. Duration: 10–20 mins

6. Arrival Claim Stage

Bags are placed onto the recirculating carousel feeding your designated claim belt. Flight information display screens (FIDS) notify passengers of the belt assignment, and final arrival scans update your tracking status to "Completed".

  • Real-time Carousel allocation display updates
  • Final arrival belt sensor scan and passenger notification
  • Baggage service desk assistance in the event of any discrepancy
