1. Academic Context & Commitment

Privacy First Architecture: AeroSim Aviation is built strictly as a client-side student simulator. We do not operate external logging servers, database backends, or data-collection pipelines.

Your privacy is preserved by architecture: all baggage tracking records, simulated accounts, contact inquiries, and irregularity filings remain entirely inside your local web browser session.

2. Client-Side Data Storage (HTML5 LocalStorage)

To preserve your simulation state across browser reloads, AeroSim uses standard browser localStorage under the following keys:

  • aerosim_user: Stores the currently active username session.
  • aerosim_users: Stores registered user records with SHA-256 hashed credentials.
  • aerosim_bags: Stores the simulation state and timeline progress for tracked bags.
  • aerosim_reports: Stores filed irregularity reports (PIR records).
  • aerosim_contacts: Stores dispatches submitted through the contact desk.
  • aerosim_notifications: Stores in-app flight telemetry alerts.
  • aerosim_theme: Saves your preferred color theme (Light / Dark).

3. Web Cryptography & Credential Hashing

In accordance with modern web engineering best practices, account passwords are never stored in plain text. When you register or change a password, your browser executes a cryptographic one-way SHA-256 digest via the native W3C window.crypto.subtle API before saving to storage.

4. Cookies & Consent

AeroSim does not set HTTP cookies. We display a standard user consent banner to inform users regarding client-side localStorage preferences. Your choice is remembered in aerosim_cookie_consent.

5. No Third-Party Trackers or Ad Networks

We do not embed third-party analytics pixels, behavioral tracking beacons, advertising cookies, or cross-site fingerprinting scripts. Web fonts are served securely via Google Fonts CDN under their standard privacy terms.

6. Your Data Rights & Immediate Account Purge

You retain absolute sovereignty over your data. You can inspect, modify, or permanently delete your simulation history at any time:

  • Visit your Profile Settings to edit details or trigger an irreversible "Delete Account" wipe.
  • Clear your browser's site data and cookies at any moment to restore AeroSim Aviation to factory defaults.
