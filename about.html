About AeroSim Aviation

Bridging the gap between passenger visibility and behind-the-scenes airport baggage handling logistics.

Our Mission & Purpose

Demystifying air transit operations through interactive simulation.

Every year, commercial airlines transport billions of checked bags worldwide. While passengers only interact with the check-in desk and arrival carousel, an intricate network of barcode sortation trays, high-speed automated conveyors, multi-level security X-ray stations, and ground apron handlers coordinate in sync to ensure bags safely reach their flight.

AeroSim Aviation was created as an educational web platform simulating the complete 6-stage IATA baggage handling lifecycle. By simulating realistic tracking tags, flight transfers, status changes, and exception reporting, this project gives aviation students and passengers transparent insights into how airport baggage sortation works in practice.

Notice: AeroSim Aviation is an educational student project for simulation and demonstration purposes only. It is not affiliated with any commercial airline or airport authority.

Project Goal & Educational Vision

Modeling airport logistics with modern web engineering standards.

The primary objective of AeroSim Aviation is to provide a transparent, accessible, and realistic educational model of the international airport baggage handling ecosystem. Commercial air passengers often experience anxiety during airport transfers due to a lack of visibility into behind-the-scenes sortation.

By implementing realistic IATA tag conventions (^[A-Z]{3}-\d{6}$), simulating optical barcode scans, conveyor redirects, ULD aircraft loading, and baggage belt distribution, AeroSim serves as both an educational case study for aviation logistics and a showcase of zero-dependency Vanilla web architecture.
