Troubleshooting Tips:

  • Confirm the 3-letter IATA origin airport code matches your boarding pass (e.g. LHR-, JFK-).
  • Ensure the tag format is strictly 3 letters, a hyphen, and 6 digits (e.g. DXB-309481).
  • If you recently checked in, telemetry may take 5–10 minutes to index at the apron sortation scanner.
  • You can report a missing or delayed bag via our Baggage Support Wizard.
